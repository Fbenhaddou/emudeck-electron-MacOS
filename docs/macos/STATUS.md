| Save snapshots and Saves page | partial | Added 2026-10-08. Snapshots of each emulator's save and state folders live in the library (`backups/saves/<emulator>/`), APFS clones where possible, sealed with a SHA-256 manifest. Taken before Dolphin/PPSSPP updates and repairs, settings resets and control changes (the change is refused if the backup fails, with a plain reason), and at most daily before launches including Console Mode. Ordered by sequence (not the clock); unchanged saves are never copied again; retention keeps 30 per emulator and never prunes the newest backup, the newest daily or the last 10 restore points. Restore verifies hashes, stages verified copies, then journals the swap; any failure or crash rolls back (at once, or at the next start), keeping anything written meanwhile as an "interrupted" backup. Dolphin's IPL firmware is excluded. Refuses while the emulator runs, including one opened outside the app. Tested with synthetic saves only (19 snapshot tests with injected failures, trigger tests, renderer tests, smoke captures); a data-loss critic and a macOS visual critic reviewed it and their findings were addressed. Three data-loss critic rounds; round 3 injected an unplug or ENOSPC at every write step of a restore (172 runs) with no loss or mixed state.
