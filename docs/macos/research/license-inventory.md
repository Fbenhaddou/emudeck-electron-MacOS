The Mac packaging configuration explicitly includes the two upstream license documents, this inventory, its research evidence documents, Electron/Chromium vendor notices, and the full React/React DOM/Scheduler MIT licenses. Generated renderer license comments remain in packaged dist. The development icon is original repository artwork generated from fixed vector geometry, with no external imagery or bundled fonts. This preservation step does not resolve conflicting provenance, third-party font/artwork obligations, or future emulator/theme redistribution terms.

Source URLs and exact inspected artifact provenance are in [component research](components.md), [emulator matrix](emulator-matrix.md), [ES-DE integration](es-de-integration.md), and [ES-DE artifact inspection](es-de-artifact.md).
